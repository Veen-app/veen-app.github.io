Veen is an iPhone app for sharing when you're free with chosen groups of friends. This page explains what data Veen processes, why, for how long, and what your rights are.

Data controller

Veen is published by Thomas LÖFFLER, a private individual, on a personal, non-professional basis. Contact: veen.app.contact@gmail.com.

The place of a slot is free text that you write: Veen never uses your location.

Why: the legal bases

  • Performance of the contract (the terms of use): your account, your friends, your groups, your slots, messages and notifications.
  • Legitimate interest: moderation (reports, blocks, text filtering), service security and fixing crashes, with no individual tracking. Reporting someone blocks them for you. An account reported by three people connected to it within 24 hours is suspended automatically; this suspension is temporary and always reviewed by a person, who restores or deletes the account. You can contest it by writing to veen.app.contact@gmail.com.

What Veen doesn't do

No ads, no trackers, no selling of data, no geolocation. No third-party analytics or crash-reporting tool is built into the app: crashes are recorded by Veen itself, with no identifier.

Service providers

  • Supabase (servers in the European Union, in Paris): database, sign-in, photos.
  • Expo (United States, member of the Data Privacy Framework): sending notifications.
  • Apple: notification service (APNs) and TestFlight.
  • Google: contact mailbox and sending sign-in codes.
  • GitHub, Inc. (United States, Data Privacy Framework participant, 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA): hosting of these pages (GitHub Pages), which records visitors' IP addresses in its technical logs, and storage of encrypted backups.

Minimum age

Veen is for people aged 15 or over.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, portability and objection. In the app: Profile > Settings > “Export my data” (a readable file of all your data) and “Delete my account” (immediate, permanent erasure). You can also write to veen.app.contact@gmail.com: reply within one month.

If you believe your rights are not respected, you can lodge a complaint with the CNIL (the French data protection authority), cnil.fr, or with the authority of your country.
