Frequently asked questions

How do I delete my account?

Profile > Settings > Delete my account. Deletion is immediate and permanent: profile, photo, friends, groups, slots and messages are erased.

How do I block someone?

Open their profile (or one of their slots), tap “…” then “Block”. You will no longer be friends, you will no longer see each other's slots and your conversation is deleted. The person isn't notified. To unblock: Profile > Settings > Blocked accounts.

How do I report content?

On a profile or a slot, tap “…” then “Report”. For a message, long-press it. Choose a reason; you can add a comment. Every report is reviewed within 24 hours.

How do I export my data?

Profile > Settings > Export my data: Veen prepares a readable file of all your data, which you can save or send. You can also ask for it by email.

I'm not receiving the sign-in code

Check the address you entered and your spam folder, then ask for a new code after a minute.
